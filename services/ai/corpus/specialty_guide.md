# Medical Specialty Selection Guide
Source: Indian Medical Association & National Medical Commission Specialty Guidelines
License: Educational Public Health Guidance

## Clinical Specialties and Associated Symptoms
- **General Physician (Internal Medicine)**:
  - Acute fever, persistent cough, cold, flu-like illness, unexplained fatigue, mild stomach pain, annual health evaluations, routine blood pressure / diabetes follow-up.
- **Cardiologist**:
  - Exertional breathlessness, irregular heartbeats, mild palpitations, chronic hypertension, post-cardiac surgery rehabilitation, cholesterol abnormalities.
- **Dermatologist**:
  - Persistent rashes, hives, eczema, psoriasis, acne, unexplained skin discoloration, fungal infections, mole evaluation.
- **Pediatrician**:
  - Infant illnesses, childhood fever, vaccination schedules, developmental milestones, pediatric nutrition, colic.
- **Orthopedist**:
  - Joint pain, arthritis, back pain, sprains, sports injuries, fractures, mobility difficulties.
- **ENT Specialist (Otolaryngologist)**:
  - Severe earache, hearing loss, chronic sinusitis, persistent sore throat, tonsillitis, vertigo, nasal polyps.
- **Gynecologist & Obstetrician**:
  - Menstrual irregularities, pregnancy care, pelvic discomfort, PCOS, fertility consultations.
- **Dentist**:
  - Toothache, gum bleeding, cavities, dental sensitivity, wisdom tooth impaction, oral hygiene.
- **Psychiatrist / Clinical Psychologist**:
  - Persistent anxiety, depression, sleep disturbances, panic episodes, mood fluctuations, stress management.
