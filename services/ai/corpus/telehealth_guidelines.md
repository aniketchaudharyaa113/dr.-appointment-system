# Telemedicine Guidelines & Consultation Ethics
Source: Telemedicine Practice Guidelines (Board of Governors in Supersession of Medical Council of India / MoHFW, Government of India)
License: Public Domain / Open Government Information Guidance

## Core Principles
1. Telemedicine consultations are valid and legal across India for registered medical practitioners (RMPs).
2. Explicit patient consent is mandatory prior to initiating any telemedicine consultation (video, audio, or text).
3. The RMP shall exercise professional discretion to determine whether a telemedicine consultation is appropriate, or whether physical in-person examination is required.
4. If a condition presents emergency red-flag symptoms, telemedicine must NOT replace immediate emergency room referral. First-aid advice may be rendered while arranging emergency transport (Dial 112 / 108).

## Prescription Rules
- Prescriptions issued via telemedicine must follow the standard format: Doctor credentials, Patient identification, Diagnosis/impression, Drug name (generic preferred), Strength, Frequency, Duration, and Instructions.
- Schedule X and habit-forming narcotics are prohibited from telemedicine prescription.
- All prescriptions must be digitally authenticated and version-controlled.
